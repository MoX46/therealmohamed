I forgot to take pictures of the second round of painting, but this time, I decided to paint and dry them lying flat. I built a drying rack which let me dry all 32 cabinet doors at once so I could paint quicker. This worked really well and the finish was much better because the self-leveling paint worked better when it was lying flat. Fortunately, the paint dried much quicker this time and didn't peel.
